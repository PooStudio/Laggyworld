Privacy Policy

We don't sell your data. We barely even store it!

Your Privacy Matters (Kind Of)

We're serious about keeping your data safe. We don't sell it to advertisers, we don't track you across the internet, and we definitely don't store it longer than we have to (usually until the next server crash).

What We Collect

  • Minecraft Username & UUID: So we know who to blame for the lag
  • IP Address: For anti-grief protection and server security
  • Playtime & Stats: For the leaderboard of who died the most
  • Chat Messages: Stored temporarily, usually deleted in the next explosion

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ Share data with advertisers
  • ❌ Track you across other websites
  • ❌ Sell your information
  • ❌ Keep logs longer than 7 days
  • ❌ Do anything sketchy with your data

Cookies & Tracking

We use basic cookies to remember your login and nothing else. No fancy tracking pixels, no advertising networks, just the essentials.

Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare for DDoS protection and Discord for authentication. Both are required for the server to work. Check their privacy policies too!

Your Rights

You can request we delete your account and all associated data. Just ask in Discord! We'll do it (eventually).

Questions or Concerns?

Join our Discord and yell at us about it: discord.gg/QUThnA4AES
