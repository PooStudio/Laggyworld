BUG REPORTS

Found a glitch? Perfect, we love those. Send your report to Discord and watch the chaos unfold.

How to Report

Send reports to our Discord with enough detail for the team to reproduce the issue. We review reports and share updates when we can.

Please avoid posting passwords, private account details, or other sensitive information.

What to Include

  • Your Minecraft username
  • What happened (the more chaotic the better)
  • Where on the map it happened
  • Screenshots or video if you have it

Before You Report

  • Check Discord for maintenance or outage announcements
  • Confirm whether the issue happens more than once
  • Include the approximate time and your Minecraft version
  • Share screenshots or video only if they contain no private information
