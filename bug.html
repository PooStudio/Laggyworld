BUG REPORTS

Found a glitch? Perfect - we love those. Send your report to Discord and watch the chaos unfold.

How to Report

Every bug report goes straight to our Discord. The more detailed, the better - but we love chaotic ones too.

We read everything. We don't promise fixes. Sometimes we turn your report into a new feature.

What to Include

  • Your Minecraft username
  • What happened (the more chaotic the better)
  • Where on the map it happened
  • Screenshots or video if you have it

Known "Features"

  • Lag - yes, it's intentional
  • Random mob spawns - also intentional
  • The server randomly restarting - it happens
  • Your base exploding for no reason - embrace it
