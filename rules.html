SERVER RULES

Keep the chaos alive by following these. Break them and face the consequences - simple as that.

01
No Real-Life Threats

Keep everything in the game. No doxxing, no IRL threats - ever.

🎮
02
No Illegal / NSFW Content

Nothing illegal or extremely offensive in chat or Discord. Period.

✨
03
No Hacks Allowed

This is a hack-free server. Modified clients that give unfair advantages = instant ban.

🔒
04
No Server Crashing

No lag machines, crash exploits, or world-breaking bugs. The lag is ours to control.

🛡️
05
Respect Staff

If staff say stop, you stop. No arguments, no appeals.

👑
06
No Advertising

Don't promote other servers or Discord communities here.

🚫
07
Griefing Is Allowed

Raiding, stealing, and griefing are all part of the game. Protect yourself.

⚔️
08
Use Common Sense

Don't ruin the server for everyone. You know what you're doing.

🧠
09
Expect Lag

It's called Laggy World for a reason. Don't complain about lag — it's the feature.

⏳

Got Questions?

Ask a staff member or hit us up in Discord for any clarifications.
