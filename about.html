OUR CHAOS

Born from frustration.
Built as a middle finger.

Back in 2023 I ran a server called “Make It”. It turned into everything I hated — pay-to-win pressure, corporate vibes, zero soul.

One day I snapped. Shut it down and said “screw this industry”. Laggy World was born as a giant middle finger to perfect servers.

Lag isn’t a bug. It’s the whole point.
